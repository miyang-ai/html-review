HTML Review · Edit & Comment on Any Page

## Summary (read from manifest, ≤132 chars)

Edit text and comment right on any web page, then copy a precise change request for your AI agent. Runs only on the tab you click.

## Category

Developer Tools (alternative: Workflow & Planning)

## Description

More and more reports, pitch decks, product pages and landing pages are written by AI as HTML. Revising them usually means switching between the browser and your editor, then describing "the third card on slide five" in words. HTML Review lets you make the change right on the page and hand it to your AI in one click.

EDIT TEXT: Press T (or Alt+T when an input method is active). Whatever is editable lights up as you hover. Click and type; press Enter to save. Changes are saved in your browser as you type and survive reloads.

COMMENT: Press C (or Alt+C). Click an element, drag to select an area, or Shift-click to pick several. Write what should change and add follow-up replies.

HAND OFF TO AI: Click "Copy change request" to get a complete prompt plus an XML change list. Every item carries an XPath, a CSS selector, the section heading, the original and new text, or your comment. Paste it into Cursor, Claude Code, Codex or any agent, and it can find the exact spot in your source.

AUTO CHECK: After the AI updates your source file, reload the page. Edits that already landed are marked as applied automatically.

Also:
- Runs only on the current tab, only when you click the icon or press Alt+Shift+R. No "read all your data on all websites" permission.
- Your edits stay in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded; the extension makes no network requests.
- The page's own shortcuts (such as arrow keys in a slide deck) keep working, and are paused while you type.
- Works on sites with a strict Content Security Policy.
- Works on local HTML files (turn on "Allow access to file URLs" on the extension details page; the extension walks you through it).
